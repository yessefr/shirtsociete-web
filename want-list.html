Sourcing

Looking for a Specific Shirt?

Every shirt we list is a single piece, so the one you want may not be online today. Tell us what you are after and we will get in touch when something matching comes in, usually before it goes on the site.

How it works

  1. Describe the shirt as precisely as you can. Club, era and size get you the best matches.
  2. We keep your request on file and check it against every shirt we source.
  3. When something fits, you hear about it first, by email.
  4. No obligation, and you can ask us to remove your request at any time.
