Shipping Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

At Shirt Société we want your vintage pieces to arrive in perfect condition and on time. Here is how we handle and ship your orders.

Processing time

Orders are processed within 2 business days. Because nearly every shirt is one of a kind, it is carefully checked and packed before dispatch. Every order is sent fully tracked, and you will receive an email with your tracking number once it ships.

How your shirt travels

Shirts are folded flat and sent with DHL. Within the Netherlands that means a tracked letterbox parcel: it is followed end to end and nobody needs to be home, because it goes straight through the door. Outside the Netherlands your shirt travels as a tracked parcel.

Orders over €500 are sent as an insured parcel requiring a signature on delivery, at no extra cost to you. DHL insurance covers up to €500; if your order is worth more than that we will contact you before dispatch to agree on additional cover.

Free shipping over €150

Orders of €150 or more ship free to every European Union destination listed below. Below that threshold, the rate for your country applies. Switzerland, Norway and the United Kingdom are outside the EU customs area and are charged at the listed rate regardless of order value.

Delivery times

Estimated delivery within the Netherlands is 1 to 3 business days after dispatch. For other destinations, estimated delivery is 3 to 10 business days, and longer where customs handling applies.

Customs and import charges

Orders to Switzerland, Norway and the United Kingdom leave the European Union customs area. Any import duties, VAT or handling fees charged on arrival are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included in the shipping rate above.

Order tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive a confirmation email with a tracking number, so you can follow your shipment on the carrier's website every step of the way.

Lost or damaged packages

If your package arrives damaged, contact us immediately at info@shirtsociete.com with photos of the damage, so we can help you with a replacement or refund. For packages lost in transit we will work with you and the carrier to resolve it.
