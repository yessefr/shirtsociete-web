Shipping

Every shirt is one of one. We pack each one with care and send it tracked.

Processing time

Orders are processed within 2 working days. Because nearly every shirt is one of a kind, it is carefully checked and packed before dispatch. You will receive an email once your order has shipped.

Shipping costs

We ship from the Netherlands. Shipping is calculated at checkout based on your destination, and confirmed before you pay.

DestinationCost

Orders over €150 (EU)Free
Free EU shipping over €150. Orders shipped within the European Union with a value over €150 are sent free of charge. This is applied automatically at checkout.

Delivery times

  • Netherlands: 3 to 7 working days
  • European Union: 5 to 10 working days
  • Rest of the world: 7 to 21 working days

All orders are sent with track and trace where available. You will receive the tracking number by email once your shirt is on its way.

Customs and import charges

Orders within the European Union have no additional customs charges. For orders outside the EU, any import duties or taxes charged by your country are your responsibility.

Packaging

Because these are original vintage and match worn shirts, each is folded carefully and shipped in protective packaging to arrive in the condition described.

Questions

If you need your shirt by a certain date, or have any question about delivery, email us at info@shirtsociete.com or message us on Instagram.
