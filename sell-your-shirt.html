Sourcing

Sell Your Football Shirts

We are always looking for original vintage and match worn football shirts. If you have a shirt sitting in a wardrobe, an old collection you no longer wear, or a match worn piece with a story behind it, we would like to see it.

What we look for

  • Original shirts only — no reproductions, retro reissues or replicas of recent seasons
  • Club and national team shirts, roughly 1970s through early 2000s
  • Match worn and player issue shirts from any era, with or without a certificate
  • Shirts with flaws are still welcome — we photograph and describe every mark honestly

How it works

  1. Fill in the form below with what you have. Photo links are welcome, but not required.
  2. We reply by email, usually within a couple of days, with an honest assessment.
  3. If it is a fit, we agree a price and arrange tracked, insured shipping.
  4. Payment is sent as soon as the shirt arrives and matches the description.

Tell us about your shirt

No photos to hand? Send the form anyway and reply to our email with pictures. You can also email us directly at info@shirtsociete.com.
