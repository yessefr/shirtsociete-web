Vintage Fussballborse: A Guide to the Fair

June 2026 · 4 min read

The Leading Vintage Football Fair in Europe

If you collect vintage football shirts anywhere in Europe, you will have heard of Vintage Fussballborse. Founded and organised by Sebastian, it has grown into one of the most respected events on the European football memorabilia calendar.

The fair is held multiple times a year across major German cities. Each edition consistently attracts over 1,000 collectors from Germany, the Netherlands, Belgium, the UK and beyond.

What to Expect

  • Dozens of exhibitors from private collectors to established dealers
  • Shirts spanning every era from the 1970s through to the early 2000s
  • Match worn and match issued pieces with proper documentation
  • Prices covering every budget, from accessible finds to serious collector pieces
  • Conversations with knowledgeable people who share the same passion for the game

Shirt Société at the Fair

Shirt Société has been a regular exhibitor since 2025, attending editions in Hamburg, Cologne, Dusseldorf and Frankfurt. Our next stand is at Leipzig on October 4th, 2026. Come and find us there.

Come and say hello. We would love to meet fellow collectors.

Follow the fair on Instagram at @vintagefussball for updates on upcoming dates and locations.
