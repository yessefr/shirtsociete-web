Original Fiorentina home shirt from 1992, one of the most talked-about kits in football history. This is the shirt that was withdrawn from sale after the repeating pattern in the design was said to resemble a swastika when viewed at certain angles. Fiorentina pulled it, and what was left became one of the most collectable Serie A shirts ever produced. Very few genuine examples circulate. Please note: the number on this shirt was applied later, not at the time of production. Size XL. Condition: very good.
