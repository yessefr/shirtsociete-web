Ajax 1982/83 Home Shirt

Ajax
  • Club Ajax
  • Season 1982/83
The Ajax home shirt from the 1982/83 season, with the broad red band that makes an Ajax shirt recognisable anywhere in the world. This was the era in which a young Marco van Basten was breaking through at the club and Johan Cruyff had returned to Amsterdam. Early eighties Ajax shirts in this condition are becoming genuinely hard to find.
