Match worn or match issued?

A match worn shirt is documented as worn by a player in a specific match. A match issued shirt was prepared for a player or a squad for a match, but cannot be shown to have been worn on the pitch. When the evidence cannot separate the two, we list the shirt as Match Worn / Issued instead of choosing the more flattering label.

How we document our shirts

Every match worn or issued shirt comes with a Shirt Passport: the player, club, season, competition and fixture, the certificate of authenticity where one exists, and any photo match or NFC tag. What is not listed in the passport, we do not claim.

Not every fixture can be confirmed

Some shirts come with a player and a season but not an exact match. We show those as Not confirmed rather than guessing. A shirt with an honest gap is worth more to a collector than one with an invented story.
